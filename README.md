A small 2D platformer built in Godot 4. Run, jump, climb ladders, and collect every coin to finish the level.

## Controls
- **Arrow keys** – move left/right, climb up/down ladders
- **Space** – jump (works off ladders too)
- **Space** (on the end screen) – restart

## How to play
Pick up all the stars scattered around the level. When the last one is collected, you'll see your final score and can jump right back in.
